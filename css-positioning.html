My Story

One morning, Tom woke up early and decided to go for a walk in the park. The weather was cool and the birds were singing.

Tom
Friend
Dog

While walking, Tom saw his old friend Sam near a large tree. They talked for a few minutes and decided to walk together.

Suddenly, they heard a small dog barking. The dog had lost its owner, so Tom and Sam gave it some water and stayed with it.

After a while, a little boy came running toward them. He was happy to find his dog again. Tom and Sam smiled and continued their walk.
